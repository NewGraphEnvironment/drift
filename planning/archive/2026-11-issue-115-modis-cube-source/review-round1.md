## Below the bar (no failure, recorded so it is not re-found)

- `R/dft_stac_cube.R` `stac_index_roles_check()`: the plural in `band role{?s} {.val {absent}}` takes its quantity from the length-1 `{.val {index}}` before it. Rendered in a scratch copy, `mndwi` on MODIS reads *"needs band role "green" and "swir16""*. The abort and its class are correct; only the wording is off. The fix is `{cli::qty(length(absent))}` placed immediately before `role{?s}`, or a literal "roles". Rule: code-check.md "A pluralisation marker takes the quantity of whatever was substituted last".
- `stac_items_datetime()` parses with `sub("Z$", "", w)` and a fixed format, so a non-`Z` offset such as `+02:00` would be ignored silently (strptime drops trailing characters). Planetary Computer writes `Z`, and `+00:00` gives the right instant, so nothing fails on this source today. Rule: code-check-spatial.md "A reader that accepts a UTC offset may not be applying it" (latent only).

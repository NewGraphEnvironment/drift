# drift: Detecting Riparian and Inland Floodplain Transitions

Fetch free global land-cover maps (Esri IO LULC, ESA WorldCover) or your
own classified rasters for any area, such as a floodplain, compare them
between two dates and across every year of a series, and turn the change
into maps, tables and change polygons. Fetch dated Sentinel-2 composites
and Esri Wayback imagery to check mapped change against, estimate
accuracy and error-adjusted area from a stratified reference sample, and
tag change areas with overlapping records such as fire perimeters or
cutblocks. Under active development.
